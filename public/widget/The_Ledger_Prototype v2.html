We hired ethical hackers and gave them nothing but our app's web address — no logins, no inside knowledge — and asked them to break in, exactly as a real attacker would. This "black box" approach is the truest test of how the app holds up from the outside. They found two serious issues; we fixed both and had them re-test to confirm the fixes worked.

Black-box (zero-knowledge) web application penetration test of the production SaaS environment. Methodology: OWASP WSTG v4.2 aligned to the OWASP Top 10 (2021). Most trust centres only show you technology. We show you all three dimensions of ISO 27001 — because the best controls in the world fail without the people and processes behind them.

01

People

Maturity · Managed

A dedicated security team owns this — it's nobody's side job. Everyone is trained every year, and we run practice drills for what to do if something goes wrong.

Dedicated ISMS function (4 FTE). Documented RACI across all control owners. Annual security-awareness training at 100% completion. Quarterly incident-response tabletop exercises. Named risk owner and IR lead.

02

Process

Maturity · Managed

Our security rules are written down, kept up to date, and actually followed day to day. We formally review our risks every quarter.

Full policy framework mapped to Annex A families. Quarterly risk-assessment cycle. Documented joiners/movers/leavers process. Change management with mandatory peer review. Version-controlled SoA, reviewed quarterly.

03

Technology

Maturity · Optimised

Our systems are watched around the clock, with automatic alerts the moment anything looks unusual — and data is encrypted everywhere it lives.

Continuous control monitoring via SIEM with tuned detections. Automated evidence collection. 24/7 alerting. Privileged-access management. Encryption at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).

05What We Don't Cover Yet

The section every other trust centre hides.

Trust is built on what you're willing to admit, not just what you're proud to show. So here, plainly, is what we haven't done — and why.

In progress

ISO 42001 — AI Management Systems. Our fraud-detection uses AI, so we're putting it through formal AI governance. Targeted for Q3 2026. Until then it follows our internal Responsible AI policy, but that policy isn't independently verified yet. Implementation underway against ISO/IEC 42001:2023 for the ML-based fraud-detection system; target certification Q3 2026. Interim governance via internal Responsible AI policy (model cards, bias testing, human-in-the-loop) — not yet third-party assured.

Not held

HITRUST. We don't handle US healthcare data, so this certification isn't relevant to us. If your use case requires it, we're likely not the right fit for that workload. No HITRUST CSF certification. No PHI processed; out of business scope. Flagged here for completeness rather than omitted.

Not held

SOC 1 / financial reporting controls. We don't perform financial reporting on behalf of our clients, so a SOC 1 report wouldn't tell you anything meaningful about us. No SOC 1 (SSAE 18) report. Meridian Pay is not a service organisation impacting client ICFR; SOC 2 is the relevant attestation.

08Questions Buyers Ask

Straight answers.

Where is our data stored?+
Your data is held in secure cloud data centres in Europe, with copies kept in a second European region so nothing is lost if one location has a problem. Your data never leaves the regions you'd expect.
Primary AWS eu-west-1 (Ireland), DR replication to eu-central-1 (Frankfurt). Data residency contractually fixed to the EU; no processing outside declared regions. Sub-processor list available in the DPA.
What happens if you have a breach?+
We have a tested plan for exactly this. We'd contain the issue, investigate it, and tell affected customers quickly — we don't hide problems. Our team practises this scenario several times a year.
Documented IR plan aligned to ISO 27035. Containment, forensic triage, and notification within 72h per GDPR Art. 33 where applicable. Quarterly tabletop exercises; annual full simulation. Post-incident reviews feed the improvement cycle.
How long do you keep our data?+
Only as long as we need it to provide the service, plus any period the law requires. When it's no longer needed, we securely delete it — and you can ask us to delete it sooner.
Retention governed by a documented schedule mapped to legal/regulatory obligations. Default purge on contract termination + statutory hold. DSAR and erasure (GDPR Art. 17) honoured within 30 days.
Who inside Meridian Pay can access my data?+
Very few people, and only when their job genuinely requires it. Access is granted on a need-to-know basis, reviewed every quarter, and every access is logged.
Least-privilege RBAC; production access gated by PAM with just-in-time elevation and MFA. Quarterly access certification. All access logged and monitored via SIEM. 0 standing production admin accounts.
